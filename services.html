How The Work Typically Moves

1.
Systems Discovery — We map the real workflow, the coordination problem, the pressure points, and the user behavior the product actually needs to support.
2.
Architecture & Interaction Design — I shape the operational logic, interface behavior, data structure, and product flow into a coherent system.
3.

4.
Deployment & Operational Adoption — Delivery includes launch planning, training, documentation, and making sure the system fits how people actually work.
