My Professional Journey

From the beginning, my greatest professional pursuit has been simple: build technology that genuinely helps people. That mission has guided over a decade of work at the intersection of operations, data, and systems design — managing inventory, aligning labor, improving financial performance, and enabling teams to do their best work.

My work started close to the friction — on the floor, inside the manual processes, at the moments where the right tool would have made all the difference. That ground-level experience shaped how I design: not from the technology outward, but from the human need inward.

My most recent development in automation software has significantly expanded that reach. What once required custom, time-intensive work can now be deployed faster, more reliably, and at greater scale — allowing me to help more people, across more contexts, with less friction in the way.

Tech Niche exists in the space of what's still discoverable — at the frontier where emerging capability meets specific, real-world need. Every application I build is a purpose-driven expression of data science: shaped by context, targeted to a problem, and designed to increase efficiency, reduce waste, surface meaningful insights, and bring structure to complexity.

What Drives My Work

  • A Mission to Help: Building technology that genuinely improves someone's work or life has been the driving force from day one — and it still is.
  • Expanded Reach Through Automation: My most recent automation work has multiplied what I can deliver — putting powerful, data-driven tools in the hands of more people, faster than before.
  • Discovery Over Convention: Tech Niche operates at the frontier — in the space between what already exists and what's still possible. The best solutions often haven't been built yet.
